How the numbers are built

Rates from claim history

For claims old enough that the outcome is known, the model measures what share of billed charges was eventually collected, split by payer, level of care and how long a claim has gone unpaid. Recent months count more, and thin cells borrow from the level-of-care average.

Recent-experience check

History is a year old by definition. The model compares cash on recent claims with what history says should have arrived by the same age, and scales the rate when a payer changes behavior, such as more MultiPlan repricing or authorization denials.

Revenue and true-up

Each claim carries an expected value: cash so far plus cash still expected. Revenue for the month is the change in that total, split into new services (charges less the implicit price concession) and the change in estimate on prior months. Gross AR, the allowance and net AR all roll forward exactly.
